IoT case study · wearables
Wearable examples

Wearable panic buttons

Built into jewellery / clothing; quickly alert a pre-designated circle of trusted contacts and the police when in distress

Safe driving internables

Ingestible pills with sensors measure blood alcohol; talk to your car, rendering it useless above the legal limit

Smart garments: slow wash

Inbuilt sensors alert you when the garment needs washing — saves water and energy

Smart garments: exact measure

Built-in sensors send body measurements to an app, which recommends clothes online that match

Non-verbal: Embrace

Smart garment that lets physical touch be transferred wirelessly between couples, recreated on their skin

Non-verbal: Mood detector

Skin-coloured patch / tattoo-like sensor tracks your mood through the day and recommends actions

Oil & gas · midstream
Midstream: problem → IoT solution
The midstream industry problem
Transporting variable volumes and grades from multiple locations to new end-users and markets
Connecting pipeline networks, sensors, leak detection, alarms and emergency shutdowns to interact seamlessly
➜
IoT solution
Data available for analysis and interpretation in real time
Would significantly reduce major risks this sector deals with
Oil & gas · downstream

Refinery shutdowns, handling various grades of crude, and changing environmental regulations

➜
IoT solution
