## Why Did Outlook and Teams Go Down for Millions at Once? 🌐💥 #shorts

Video: visuals/news-ms-outage/out/news-ms-outage.mp4

### Description

This week a global Microsoft outage knocked out Outlook, Teams and other services for hours, and Microsoft said it wasn't aware of any malicious activity behind it. So how does one company's problem hit millions of people at once? Big cloud services share core systems like sign-in, configuration and storage. A single bad change or an overloaded component can spread across all of them, and when millions of apps keep retrying at the same time, the load gets even worse. Engineers fix it by finding what changed and rolling it back, which is why big companies roll out changes slowly, region by region.

## Your AI Chats Aren't as Private as You Think 🔓💬 #shorts

Video: visuals/news-ai-chats-leak/out/news-ai-chats-leak.mp4

### Description

Notion confirmed that some Notion AI chats were exposed through a breach at Fireworks AI, a company Notion used behind the scenes for its Insights feature. Attackers broke into a cloud provider that hosted Fireworks' machines. Notion says fewer than 1% of workspaces may be affected and the chats weren't labelled with names, but conversations can still contain personal details, so it advised users to change any passwords they had pasted into Notion AI. The lesson: the app you use often shares your data with other companies you've never heard of. Never paste passwords, API keys or private data into AI chats.

## Apple Refused Touchscreen Macs for 15 Years. Why Now? 💻👆 #shorts

Video: visuals/news-touch-mac/out/news-touch-mac.mp4

### Description

Apple's first touchscreen MacBook Pro went on sale on 6 November 2026. For years Apple said no: in 2010 Steve Jobs argued that touching a vertical screen gets tiring fast, and the Mac was built around a precise mouse pointer with small buttons. What changed? Macs now run on the same kind of chips as iPads and can run iPad apps, macOS has become more touch-friendly, and Apple says apps like Microsoft 365, Notion and Canva are getting touch updates. Whether people will actually reach for the screen is the big question.
